How it works

  1. Share your tripYour flight date and time, terminal, and where in Ann Arbor you're going.
  2. Get matchedWe group 2–4 riders landing or departing within about an hour of each other.
  3. Ride togetherOne person books the Uber or Lyft, and everyone pays back their share on Venmo.

Why this exists

470+DTW ride requests posted by UM students since Jan 2025
1 in 4found a co-rider within an hour of their trip
~$100for a solo late-night Uber to Ann Arbor

From an analysis of UM's #dtw-airport-carpool Slack channel. Sharing with two others cuts your fare, and your trip's emissions, by about two-thirds.

Questions

Is this an app?

Not yet. During the pilot, matching is done by hand by the person building Pairway. What we learn here decides what the app does.

Who sees my information?

Only the riders in your matched group, and only your first name, email and (if you add it) phone number. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

What if my flight is delayed?

Message your group. During the pilot there's no fee and no penalty if plans change. Just let your co-riders know.

Does it cost anything?

The matching is free. You pay only your share of the ride.
